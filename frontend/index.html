SciGraph Documentation & Quick Reference
How to get the most out of your scientific intelligence platform

1. Multimodal Document Ingestion

Upload scientific PDFs via the Research Library. The pipeline uses MinerU to detect layouts, extract mathematical equations, convert tables to markdown, and run OCR on embedded diagrams.

2. Hybrid Retrieval Modes in Assistant

  • Mix (Hybrid): Combines vector similarity with multi-hop knowledge graph traversal. Recommended for most scientific questions.
  • Global: Synthesizes high-level research questions by querying entity community summaries.
  • Local: Precision lookup anchored around specific named concepts, authors, or experimental techniques.
  • Naive: Standard dense vector cosine search.

3. Keyboard Shortcuts

Press / anywhere to focus global search. In the AI Assistant, press Enter to submit a query and Shift+Enter for a newline.
